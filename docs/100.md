## Done

## Left
### Gameplay
- ✅ **Battle Arena and Ring Out from the Arena Modes menu, two players** (2026-10-27, `tools/regress/live_run.sh` steered
  by looking at its frames; pad 2 is `SSA_PRESS2=start:5000`, the second figure goes on the portal through the Toy Box's
  Player 2 prompt): Rampart Ruins (Battle Arena) and the Ring Out arena played about a minute each with both HUDs, Eruptor
  against Terrafin, 24-30 fps on a machine at load 70, no fault. Both modes answer "You need two players" without pad 2.
- 🔧 **Team Survival, Rival Survival and Solo Survival from the menu**: every survival arena is **locked on a new save**
  ("Continue the story to unlock soon"; the zone's unlock is a story level, `_levelCompletedToUnlockArena`, in the save), so
  they cannot be entered from the menu until the story has unlocked one. Not played: needs a save with an arena unlocked
  (the story's levels, below) or a way to set that save field.
- 🔧 **The story through its own flow**: a new game plays the tutorial and its cutscenes into the first Barrens level
  ("Woodburrow"; about 6 minutes of a scripted walk and attack run, no fault, Eruptor with his HUD). A scripted blind
  bot cannot beat the chapters; the other story levels were only started alone ([history.md](history.md), "Zone tour").
- ❌ A Swap Zone entered from its story level (its staging area and intro then load with it): needs the story.
- ❌ The ultrawide HUD mask left of the portrait, Player 2 with two swappers, the saves read back after a restart, the
  short Skylander intro, the ultrawide HUD at the window edges, the movie sweep: not done. A Player 1 swapper through the Toy Box was tested (`tools/regress/swapper_toybox.sh`); Player 2 with a swapper remains untested.
- 🔧 **Long falls at 60 fps** (lessons ticket): the stuck-falling check (`sub_82A4A828`) has its distance threshold scaled by
  30 / fps (`games/swapforce/fall_check.cpp`); the hook runs at `--fps-limit 60` (`[fall]` line, component scale 1);
  a scripted long fall at 60 fps was not made.
