SSA is the reference ([games/ssa/docs/parity.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/spyros-adventure/blob/main/docs/parity.md)); copy the rows of
[Giants' table](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/giants/blob/main/docs/parity.md). Legend (every game): ✅ done, 🔧 in progress, ⏸ paused, ❌ not done.

Proofs are runs of this build on macOS (Metal, offscreen, the tutorial of the recipe in
[testing.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/getting-started/testing.md#swap-force-to-the-tutorial-in-25-60-s-swapforce), isolated
folders, 2026-10-27). **[V]** = run and seen, **[?]** = read in code or not run.

## Runtime (in game)
| Feature | SSA | Swap Force | Where documented |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultrawide (Hor+) | the camera-aspect global and wide targets (SSA hooks) | ✅ the scene's render-target pass `sub_8258EBE8` (at full resolution, the default since 2026-10-27, the scene is 720 high at the window's shape: 1696x720 for 2560x1080; with `original_resolution=1`, 1040x620 becomes the window's shape at the same pixel count, 1232x512 for 2560x1080 and 3440x1440), the world camera `sub_82B992A8` (the viewport object's aspect at +8, for that call only), the indexed composite quad (`GAME_ULTRAWIDE_COMPOSITE_INDEXED`); 2560x1080 and 3440x1440 shots in the tutorial, 16:9 unchanged (194 draws at 1040x620, no `[ultrawide]` line), the movies and menus centred with bars; limit: the HUD's dark stencil mask (drawn into the scene) is not centred and shows as a black shape left of the portrait | [ultrawide.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/ultrawide.md) ("Swap Force"), [boot.md](boot.md) |
| HD texture packs | generic dump and replace | ✅ 318 textures dumped to `<data>/Swapforce/textures-dump/` in a run to the tutorial; the pack folder is the game's (`<data>/Swapforce/textures/`, log `1 replacement textures in .../Swapforce/textures`); the 872x400 logo replaced by a solid colour and seen on the legal screen; the text bitmaps and buttons are not textures (next row) | [texture_packs.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/texture_packs.md) |
| Button glyphs (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, keyboard) | the game's glyph textures replaced | ✅ the fonts' `button` images (the source of the buttons the CPU text renderer copies into strings) are found in guest memory and redrawn in place: "Press (X) to Attack." shot in the 5 styles, "Select"/"Back" in PlayStation; `auto` follows the device; harness check; shoulder buttons drawn; ✅ the title's "PRESS START" / "APPUYER SUR START", the one text that spells a button, shows the **Start button's picture** in every style (keycap, Options, PS3 Start, +, Menu, the 360's ▶; live on a switch; shots; harness check); ✅ one style per pad family (PS4/PS5, PS3, Switch by position, Xbox One, 360) and the game's own key bindings (`[swapforce.input]`); ❌ the 3D world prompts are meshes (letters are geometry), not replaced; [?] the Turbo race A texture is in the table, never seen; a string already on screen changes at its next composition | [input.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/xbox360/input.md) ("Glyphs"), [art.md](art.md) ("Button pictures") |
| Pipelines compiled **during the loading screens** | the baseline from a 70-level tour, compiled when each `mission*.str` opens | ✅ 738-pipeline baseline (the 195-zone tour with Eruptor, then Blast Zone, Scorp and seven more figures, 2026-10-28), waited for at every `common_ents_zone.pak` open; draw-time builds `brn2` 130 -> 3, Blast Zone in `brn1` 21 -> 1 | [pipeline_cache.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/pipeline_cache.md) ("Swap Force") |
| Fast loads: the game's decompression as host code | zlib's `inflate`: `mission.str` 3.4 -> 0.3-0.5 s | ✅ LZMA (the LZMA SDK's decoder loop, 2026-10-28): `brn2` 5.6-8.7 -> 4.9-5.3 s, the decoders' time halved; zlib is linked but unused | [lzma.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/engines/alchemy/lzma.md) |
| The render thread's CPU | waits for the blank in the Swap hook | ✅ its spin on the last swap made a wait (`swap_wait.cpp`, 2026-10-28): 13.8-14.6 -> 9.5 ms of CPU a frame at 30 fps | [renderer_threads.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/gpu/renderer_threads.md) ("Swap Force's swap wait") |
| 60 fps option | the engine's measured frame time (`--fps-limit 60`) | ✅ 52-56 fps in the tutorial and `barrens/brn2` at 60 (29.8-30.0 at 30), the game's own clock at 1.00 game second per wall second in both (2026-10-27). The title paces itself by the interval it reads from its device (2: a fixed 1/30 s step and two blanks in its own code); at 60 the runtime writes 1 there before each swap and the engine times its frames ; the stuck-falling check's threshold is scaled by 30 / fps above 30 (`fall_check.cpp`, reached at 60: `[fall]` line); ❌ a scripted long fall at 60 was not made; below 60 the frames are shown every 2 blanks (steady 30) until they fit again, which ended the judder of the user's tutorial at ~45 fps (2026-10-28, [video_vblank.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/runtime/xbox360/video_vblank.md#pacing-waitforflippresentinterval-in-the-swap-hook)) | [boot.md](boot.md), [settings.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/getting-started/settings.md) |
| Toy Box (My Skylanders, seats, quick-swap wheel) | generic ImGui UI | ✅ generic; the one-seat-per-player limit for swappers is in [status.md](status.md) | [history.md](history.md) ("Figures") |
| Movies | Bink 1 on the GPU, the intro skip, 16:9 bars | ✅ Bink 2 drawn on the GPU; 16:9 with black bars in an ultrawide window (logos and `fmv01` seen at 2560x1080) | [bink.md](https://github.com/arkeyan-forge/forge/blob/main/docs/engines/lux/bink.md) |
